Hoppa

Content and SEO Specialist
2025 to 2026

An interactive stargazing guide built from search data

Hoppa's article on the best places to stargaze in Europe was a long read. I used query fan-out analysis to map the questions people ask around it, about travel, astronomy, the best season and where to go, and used them to shape both the article and an interactive guide inside it.

Readers choose how they like to travel, as a serious observer, a photographer, a family or a nature lover, and the guide shows them a destination with its nearest airport and transfer time.

  • Mapped the fan-out data to the page structure and the routes through the guide
  • Built the guide so readers who don't want the whole article can find their spot in a few clicks
  • Wrote each section to answer 1 question clearly, for Google and for ChatGPT
  • Explore the guide on hoppa.com (opens in a new tab)

Elife Transfer

Content and SEO Specialist
2025 to 2026

380+ pages moved to a new CMS with their rankings intact

Elife was moving its website to Strapi to give its markets in the US and Asia a cleaner setup that could grow. I led the content and SEO side of the move: more than 380 pages in English, Spanish and Chinese, most of them airport pages, plus the homepage and the key service pages.

  • Audited the pages before they moved, to protect the rankings they already had
  • Updated content where search intent had changed
  • Rebuilt page structures in Strapi, from components and layouts to metadata
  • Worked with the developers on site structure, XML sitemaps, hreflang and schema
  • Designed page templates that could scale without every page reading the same

The move finished without disrupting live pages, and rankings and organic traffic held. The team now has a cleaner content system it can keep building on.
